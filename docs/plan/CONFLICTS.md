# SPECTRA — Part I / Part II conflicts

Status: **audit complete, decisions outstanding.**

## What this is

Part II of the master prompt overrides Part I wherever they conflict. This is the audit of where that actually happens. Twenty agents each compared one Part II section against its Part I counterparts.

| Class | Raw | After dedupe | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| RESOLVED | 101 | 100 | Part II explicitly overrides and says so. Follow Part II. No decision needed. |
| SILENT | 213 | 213 | Part II contradicts Part I **without** marking it an override. An implementer reading Part I alone builds the superseded behaviour. |
| UNRESOLVED | 140 | 140 | The two disagree and Part II does not settle it. **These need a human decision.** |

Raw counts exceed deduplicated counts because several findings surface in more than one section audit. The `Also in` column records where else a finding was reported.

### What the volume means

A specification that needs this many reconciliations is not yet buildable as written. That is the expected state after a hardening pass was bolted onto a completed draft, and it is why Part II exists — but it means the next work item is reconciliation, not implementation. The SILENT list in particular should be pushed back into the Part II source files as explicit `OVERRIDES Part I:` lines before any engine section is implemented.

The unabridged audit, with full quotations from both sides, is in `CONFLICTS_FULL.md`.
